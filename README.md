# TMTDC 2026 — Novel Ride Design

We are competing at the **Toronto Metropolitan Thrill Design Competition (TMTDC)** in Orlando, Florida, in the **Novel Ride Design** challenge.

TMTDC is an invitational themed-entertainment design competition organized by Toronto Metropolitan University and hosted at Universal Orlando Resort with Universal Creative. In 2026, teams from 35 universities were invited to the November invitational. Sponsors cover participation so students can design with industry mentors in a realistic setting, instead of competing only for cash prizes.

## Novel Ride Design

Novel Ride Design is our challenge. It is a short design sprint: we conceive an original ride and present it to industry jurors. The work has to hold story, guest experience, and engineering together — how the ride moves people, why that motion belongs to the attraction, and how the system stays safe, buildable, and operable.

This repository is where that work lives.
